# Payment Gateway Outage Report

Document ID: INC-2025-0042
Department: Payments
Document type: Incident
Access level: Internal
Created date: 2025-02-14

## Root Cause

The payment authorization service experienced elevated failures after a connection pool limit was reached during a traffic spike. A retry storm amplified load on the downstream gateway.

## Impact

Card authorization success fell for 27 minutes. No payment data was lost. The incident was resolved by increasing pool capacity and disabling aggressive retries.

## Corrective Actions

Add pool saturation alerts, use exponential backoff with jitter, and load test peak traffic before releases.
