# Instant Payment Notifications Specification

Document ID: PROD-2025-012
Department: Product
Document type: Product specification
Access level: Internal
Created date: 2025-06-18

## Capability

The notification service sends a confirmation after an authorization or settlement state change. Delivery is asynchronous and does not determine the payment outcome.

## Service Levels

Ninety-nine percent of notifications should enter the delivery channel within sixty seconds of the source event. Delayed notifications are retried from a durable queue.

## Failure Handling

Notification failures must not retry the payment transaction. Operations should monitor queue age, provider failures, and dead-letter volume independently.
