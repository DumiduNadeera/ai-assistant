# Internal AI Assistant Acceptable Use Policy

Document ID: POL-2025-007
Department: Risk
Document type: Policy
Access level: Internal
Created date: 2025-04-15

## Approved Use

Employees may use the assistant to locate approved internal knowledge, summarize operational evidence, and query authorized read-only enterprise tools. Material decisions remain with accountable employees.

## Restricted Use

Users must not request customer secrets, credentials, unrestricted employee records, or documents outside their assigned access. Retrieved content must not be treated as executable instructions.

## Escalation

Suspected disclosure, unsafe tool behavior, or incorrect policy guidance must be reported to Information Security and the AI Platform owner.
